Accessibility and journey test: Northwind Fixtures

Prepared by Harbour Lane Digital for Northwind Fixtures · Tested 17 October 2026 · Run run_20261016-232000_s4mp2e

Summary

7 issues found against WCAG 2.2 AA in tested pages, 1 of them high severity.

On 17 October 2026 we tested 9 pages of Northwind Fixtures, at http://127.0.0.1:4173, with 2 test accounts. We checked the pages against WCAG 2.2 level AA, the current accessibility guidelines, and checked whether customers can sign up, log in, add to cart, and check out.

Log in and add to cart worked. Checkout did not work for the test account: at step 26 of 26, the page should have said "Thanks for your order!" but said "Payment was successful" instead, after the site's server answered 500 (an error on its side) to POST /api/orders. Sign-up was not tested this round.

We found 7 issues: 1 high, 6 medium, and 0 low. Fix these first: checkout did not work (high), images have no description (medium) and text is too faint to read easily (medium). Each issue below says where it happens, why it matters to your customers, and how to fix it.

Automated checks find only some accessibility problems. 51 of the 55 WCAG 2.2 A and AA criteria need a person to review, such as keyboard use and whether image descriptions make sense. They are marked "Needs manual review" in the criteria table, and none of them is counted as passed.

A-02 · Medium · Text is too faint to read easily

Customers with low vision, older customers, and anyone on a phone in sunlight struggle to read pale text, including prices and stock levels.
How to fix it
Darken the text or lighten the background until the contrast is at least 4.5 to 1 (3 to 1 for large text). The saved results give each ratio.

Medium. Starting grade from the automated check, which rates it serious.

A-03 · Medium · Buttons have no name

Where
  • product-page (/product/1), 2 places, such as <button id="decrease-quantity" class="btn btn-link">-</button>
  • sign-in (/login), 1 place, such as <button class="btn btn-outline-secondary"></button>
  • sign-up (/register), 1 place, such as <button class="btn btn-outline-secondary"></button>
Why it matters to your customers
A screen reader announces only "button", so customers cannot tell which one adds to cart, removes an item, or shows a password.
How to fix it
Give each button visible text, or an aria-label for icon-only buttons, such as aria-label="Increase quantity".
Severity
Medium. Graded by the reviewer: A screen-reader customer can still add one of something, but cannot knowingly change the quantity.

A-04 · Medium · Form fields have no label

Where
  • cart (/checkout), 1 place, such as <input data-test="product-quantity" type="number">
  • catalogue (/), 1 place, such as <input data-test="search-query" class="form-control">
  • product-page (/product/1), 1 place, such as <input data-test="quantity" type="number" value="1">
Why it matters to your customers
Screen reader users hear "edit text" with no idea what to type, so forms such as checkout or sign-up become guesswork.
How to fix it
Connect a visible label to each field with <label for="...">, or give the field an aria-label.
Severity
Medium. Graded by the reviewer: The fields can still be used, but a screen-reader customer has to guess which one searches or sets the quantity.

Scope and limits

  • This report lists issues found against WCAG 2.2 AA in tested pages. It covers only the pages and journeys named in it, as they were on 17 October 2026. Pages that are not listed were not tested, and later changes to the site are not covered.
  • It is not a statement that the site has no other accessibility issues, and it is not legal advice.
  • This is not a security test. Nothing was done to look for vulnerabilities or to break into the site, and the results say nothing about how secure it is.
  • Testing was done with the site owner's written authorisation, given by Ada Owner (Owner), record fixture-shop-owner, in force until 2027-01-12.
  • Only test accounts were used. Passwords, codes, and personal details are hidden in every saved file.
  • 51 criteria marked "Needs manual review" were not checked by a person in this round. No criterion is marked as passed: where the automated checks found nothing, the report says only that.
  • Left out on purpose: sign-up (Sign-up creates a new account on every run, so it is left out of the monthly check.).
  • Agreed exclusions: Real personal or payment details
